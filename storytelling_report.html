Behind every number in a dataset is a student — their effort, their struggles, and their potential. This report cleans raw, messy data and listens to what it has to say.

Chapter 1The Messy Reality — Before Cleaning

Real-world data is never perfect. Our raw dataset of 210 records arrived with a host of problems — missing scores, inconsistent gender labels, impossible ages, and duplicate entries. Before any insight could be drawn, the data had to be fixed.

ConclusionThe Story the Data Tells

This dataset tells the story of a class that is performing adequately on average, but hiding a struggling minority beneath that average. The data reveals:

  • Data cleaning is essential — 10 duplicates and dozens of missing values could have skewed every finding.
  • Subject difficulty varies — English consistently underperforms Math and Science.
  • A significant portion of students (20.5%) need academic support urgently.
  • Gender differences exist but are minor — not a primary concern.
  • Attendance alone does not determine success — learning habits and self-study play a role.
